# Culture, Food, and Dining Etiquette

Singapore is a multicultural nation where food culture sits at the center of everyday life. The following summarizes key points visitors should know.

## Hawker Centres
- Open-air food courts serving a wide range of local dishes (e.g., Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, char kway teow). Cascading rows of independent stalls, with communal seating; typically cashless-friendly now.

## Halal and Dietary Needs
- Halal-certified eateries are labeled; neighborhoods like Kampong Glam host many halal options. Vegetarian and vegan choices are increasingly available in central districts.

## Tipping and Service
- Tipping is not customary. Restaurants often include a service charge and GST; rounding up for convenience is fine but not required.

## Etiquette
- Public behavior is generally formal and polite. Quiet conversation on public transport is preferred; queue discipline is strongly observed (at MRT platforms, buses, and food stalls).

## Food Safety
- Singapore enforces strict food safety standards; hawker centres and restaurants are regularly inspected and graded.

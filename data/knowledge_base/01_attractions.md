# Singapore — Major Attractions

Singapore packs a wide variety of attractions into a compact island city-state. Below are short, practical descriptions of the key places visitors often include in an itinerary. Citations are preserved via the `source_title` header above.

## Marina Bay Sands
- Integrated resort complex with observation deck, infinity pool (hotel guests), high-end shopping, and the ArtScience Museum. The Marina Bay waterfront promenade and Merlion Park lie nearby, making this a central hub for evening city views and light shows.

## Gardens by the Bay
- Worlds of flora and horticultural displays including the cooled Conservatories (Flower Dome & Cloud Forest) and the Supertree Grove. The nightly Garden Rhapsody light show is visitor-favorite.

## Sentosa Island
- Leisure island with beaches, Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, and multiple family-oriented attractions. Accessible by Sentosa Express, cable car, or road.

## Merlion Park
- Iconic waterfront statue combining a lion head with a fish body; popular photo spot close to the Esplanade and Marina Bay.

## Singapore Botanic Gardens
- A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring the National Orchid Garden, lakes, and wide lawns for picnics and strolls.

## Chinatown
- Heritage district with temples (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple), shophouses, markets, and hawker food. Cultural walking routes highlight architecture and community history.

## Little India
- Vibrant neighborhood for textiles, temples (Sri Veeramakaliamman), and South Asian cuisine. Tekka Centre is a popular market and hawker hub.

## Kampong Glam
- Malay-Arab quarter anchored by Sultan Mosque; boutique shops, halal eateries, and the colorful Haji Lane.

## Practical tips
- Best times: Early morning for gardens and cooler outdoor exploration; evenings for waterfront, light shows, and hawker centres.
- Mobility: Many attractions are MRT-accessible; some (Sentosa) require a short transfer.

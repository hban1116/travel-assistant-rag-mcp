# Sample Itineraries — 3-day and 5-day Options

Below are sample day-by-day itineraries that balance outdoor and indoor activities, with suggestions for indoor alternatives in case of rain.

## 3-Day Sample — Classic Highlights

### Day 1 — Marina Bay & Gardens
- Morning: Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome & Cloud Forest). Midday: Lunch at nearby hawker centre. Afternoon: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark and ArtScience Museum. Evening: Marina Bay waterfront light show.

### Day 2 — Cultural Districts
- Morning: Chinatown walking tour, Buddha Tooth Relic Temple. Afternoon: Little India and Tekka Centre. Evening: Kampong Glam and Haji Lane.

### Day 3 — Sentosa & Beach
- Morning–Afternoon: Sentosa Island (beach, S.E.A. Aquarium or Universal Studios). Evening: Return to VivoCity or Clarke Quay for dinner.

### Indoor Alternatives (if rainy)
- Swap outdoor Gardens by the Bay for Cloud Forest + ArtScience Museum, spend more time at indoor Sentosa attractions, or visit the National Gallery Singapore.

## 5-Day Sample — Leisurely Pace
- Day 1: Marina Bay + Gardens
- Day 2: Chinatown + Little India + Kampong Glam
- Day 3: Sentosa (full day) or indoor theme park
- Day 4: Botanic Gardens + Orchard Road shopping
- Day 5: Day trips, museums, or culinary tour of hawker centres

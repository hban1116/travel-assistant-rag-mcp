# Transport in Singapore — MRT, Buses, Taxis, and More

Singapore's transit system is efficient, frequent, and widely used by locals and visitors. This document summarizes the practical transport options and tips.

## MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)
- Extensive rail network covering most central and suburban areas. Lines are color-coded and clearly signed. Stations have lifts and accessible routes at most interchanges.

## Buses
- Dense bus network complements the MRT for short and cross-suburban trips; bus stops are frequent and buses are air-conditioned.

## EZ-Link / Contactless Payment
- EZ-Link cards and contactless payments (credit cards, mobile wallets) are accepted on MRT and buses. Topping up can be done at stations and convenience stores.

## Taxi & Ride-hailing
- Taxis are widely available; Grab is the popular ride-hailing app in Singapore for meter and private hire rides.

## Changi Airport to City
- Changi Airport connects to the city by MRT (East-West Line via Tanah Merah), airport shuttle, taxis, and private transfers. Travel times to the city centre typically range 20–40 mins.

## Accessibility and Peak Times
- Peak hours are morning (07:30–09:30) and evening (17:30–19:30); plan accordingly. MRT offers priority seating and extensive signage.
